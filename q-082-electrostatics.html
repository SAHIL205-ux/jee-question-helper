Electric field due to 2 μC charge at 3 m, k=9×10⁹, is?

Answer: 2000 N/C

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 — E=kq/r².
Step 2 — =9×10⁹×2×10⁻⁶/9=2000 N/C.
