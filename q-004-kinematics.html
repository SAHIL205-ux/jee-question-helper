A particle has u=6 m/s and a=2 m/s² over 5 s. Find displacement.

Answer: 55 m

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 — s=ut+½at².
Step 2 — s=6(5)+½(2)(25)=55 m.
⚡ JEE shortcut:
Use s=ut+½at².
