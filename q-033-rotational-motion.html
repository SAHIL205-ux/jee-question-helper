JEE Main · Hard · Original JEE-style practice question

Angular velocity changes from 2 to 10 rad/s in 4 s. Find angular acceleration.

Answer: 2 rad/s²

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 — α=(ω−ω₀)/t.
Step 2 — 8/4=2 rad/s².
⚡ JEE shortcut:
α=Δω/Δt.
