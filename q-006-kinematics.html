A particle covers 30 m in 3 s from rest with constant acceleration. Find acceleration.

Answer: 20/3 m/s²

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 — s=½at².
Step 2 — 30=½a(9), so a=60/9=20/3.
⚡ JEE shortcut:
From rest: a=2s/t².
