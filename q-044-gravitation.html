Gravitation

JEE Advanced · Easy · Original JEE-style practice question

At height h=R above a planet surface, what is g compared with surface value?

Answer: g/4

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 — Distance from center becomes 2R.
Step 2 — g' = GM/(2R)² = g/4.
⚡ JEE shortcut:
Use distance from center, not height alone.
