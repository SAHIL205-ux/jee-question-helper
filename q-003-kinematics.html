A car moving at 20 m/s stops uniformly in 5 s. Find its acceleration.

Answer: −4 m/s²

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 — a=(v-u)/t.
Step 2 — a=(0−20)/5=−4 m/s².
⚡ JEE shortcut:
Stopping acceleration = −u/t.
