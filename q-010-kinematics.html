JEE Advanced · Easy · Original JEE-style practice question

A body moves 12 m in the first 2 s and 20 m in the next 2 s with uniform acceleration. Find acceleration.

Answer: 2 m/s²

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 — First: 12=2u+2a.
Step 2 — Next interval: 20=2u+6a.
Step 3 — Subtract: 8=4a, so a=2 m/s².
⚡ JEE shortcut:
For equal intervals, use displacement difference = aτ².
