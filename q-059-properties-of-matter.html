For an ideal fluid, if speed increases along a streamline, static pressure generally does what?

Answer: Decreases

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 — Bernoulli: P+½ρv²+ρgh=constant.
Step 2 — At same height, larger v means smaller P.
⚡ JEE shortcut:
Higher speed → lower static pressure at same height.
