A train changes speed from 10 to 30 m/s in 4 s uniformly. Find average speed.

Answer: 20 m/s

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 — For uniform acceleration, average speed=(u+v)/2.
Step 2 — (10+30)/2=20 m/s.
⚡ JEE shortcut:
Uniform acceleration → v_avg=(u+v)/2.
