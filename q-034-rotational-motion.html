JEE Advanced · Easy · Original JEE-style practice question

A torque of 6 N m acts on a body with angular acceleration 3 rad/s². Find moment of inertia.

Answer: 2 kg m²

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 — τ=Iα.
Step 2 — I=6/3=2 kg m².
⚡ JEE shortcut:
I=τ/α.
