A particle of mass 2 kg is at radius 3 m. Find its moment of inertia about axis through center.

Answer: 18 kg m²

Step-by-step solution

Step 1 — I=mr².
Step 2 — 2×9=18 kg m².
⚡ JEE shortcut:
Point mass: I=mr².
